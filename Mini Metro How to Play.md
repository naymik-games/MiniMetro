# Mini Metro: How to Play

## Goal

- Deliver as many passengers as you can. Each one delivered is 1 point.
- The game ends when any station stays overcrowded long enough for its red ring to fill.

## Basics

- **Stations** come in shapes: circle, square, triangle, plus, hexagon and star. New ones appear over time.
- **Passengers** wait at stations with an icon showing the shape they want to reach. They want a *different* shape than the station they're at.
- A passenger is delivered when their train stops at **any** station of the matching shape.

## Controls

- **Draw a line:** press on a station and drag to others.
- **Extend a line:** drag from either end of an existing line.
- **Add a stop mid-line:** drag out from the middle of a line to splice stations in.
- **Shorten a line:** drag back over the previous station, or back along the track.
- **Make a loop:** with 3 or more stations, drag from one end and release on the other end.
- **Remove a station from a line:** right-click it. The neighbours get joined up.
- **Delete a line:** shorten it until fewer than 2 stations remain. Its riders go back to the nearest station.
- **Speed:** pause, normal, fast and ultra (10x) are available. Use pause to plan.

## Upgrades

- **Line:** unlocks another line slot, up to the map's maximum.
- **Locomotive:** drag it onto a line to add a train.
- **Carriage:** drag it onto a train for +6 capacity (a train holds 6, with up to 2 carriages).
- **Station upgrade:** click the icon, then click a station. The station holds 4 more waiting passengers and trains stop there half as long. Right-click cancels.
- **Bridge:** needed to run track over water. Each bridge is used up by the water crossings on your lines.
- **Weekly reward:** every 7 days (70 seconds at normal speed) the game pauses and you pick an upgrade.

## Overcrowding

- A station is fine up to **6 waiting passengers** (10 if upgraded).
- Above that, a red ring starts filling. It takes about 25 game-seconds to fill, and that ends the game.
- The ring drains at double speed once the queue drops back under the limit.

## Demand Events

- After about a minute, orange pulsing rings mark a **venue** station (plus, hexagon or star) for 30 seconds.
- During an event, passengers are much more likely to want that shape.
- Every other station also generates passengers faster.

## Tips and Tricks

- **Connect every shape early.** Passengers only spawn wanting shapes that currently exist, so a shape you haven't linked will leave people stranded.
- **Build interchanges.** The routing prefers a direct ride, but it will use a transfer if the route is shorter. Stations shared by two or more lines act as hubs.
- **Watch the direction of travel.** Passengers only board a train heading toward the next station on their route.
- **Use loops for busy circuits.** Loop trains only run forward and never reverse, so they keep a steady flow with no dead ends.
- **Fix the biggest queue first.** Add a locomotive to the line serving the fullest station before adding more track.
- **Add carriages and locomotives.** Carriages raise capacity at no extra track cost, while more locomotives cut wait times.
- **Save station upgrades for hubs.** The extra buffer and faster stops matter most where lines meet.
- **Spend bridges carefully.** Planning routes to cross as little water as possible leaves bridges for when you really need them.
- **Rare shapes spawn rarely.** Plus, hexagon and star unlock after 3 to 6 stations have appeared. Keep them well connected, since events spike demand for them.
- **Extend as you grow.** A new station spawns about every 15 game-seconds, so extend your lines regularly to pick them up.
- **Re-draw if needed.** If a line isn't working, reshape it. You get your line slot back if you delete it.
